# Meikai

A platform where readers upload their own Japanese manga and read it with hover-to-look-up text.

## Language

**Reader**:
A person with a meikai account who uploads and reads manga.
_Avoid_: User, customer, account

**Library**:
A Reader's private collection of uploaded manga, visible only to that Reader.
_Avoid_: Catalog, collection, shelf

**Series**:
A single manga title in a Reader's Library, grouping its Volumes.
_Avoid_: Title, manga, work

**Volume**:
One uploaded archive of a Series, read as an ordered sequence of Pages.
_Avoid_: Book, tankōbon, upload

**Volume status**:
Where a Volume is in its lifecycle: Uploading, Processing, Ready (readable) or Rejected (the upload failed validation, and the Reader is told why).
_Avoid_: State, stage, job status

**Page**:
One image within a Volume.
_Avoid_: Image, scan

**Reading Progress**:
How far a Reader has read in a Volume: the last Page reached, and whether the Volume is finished.
_Avoid_: Bookmark, position, history

**Text Box**:
A region of a page holding one piece of recognised Japanese text, positioned so it can be overlaid on the image for lookup.
_Avoid_: Bubble, caption, OCR block
